This site is the live demo of [Elucidate](https://github.com/elucidatejs/elucidate), an open-source
publishing tool for scientific articles on the web. It is also the starter template that
`npm create elucidate` copies, so everything you see here is yours to change.

Pages like this one are ordinary Markdown or MDX files in `src/pages/`. They get the same typography
as articles, without the byline and appendix.

## Contact

Questions, corrections and ideas are welcome as
[issues on GitHub](https://github.com/elucidatejs/elucidate/issues).
